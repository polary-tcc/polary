Privacy and site use

How this version of the site handles information from your visit.

Contact form

The form prepares a message in your email app. This version does not send or store its fields on a Polary server. The message is only sent when you complete the process in your email app. Avoid including passwords or sensitive information.

Theme preference

Your browser may store your light or dark theme preference locally. You can remove it through your browser’s site data settings.

Measurement and hosting

This version does not embed external analytics or advertising services. Generic interactions may be recorded temporarily in page memory, without form fields. The hosting platform may process technical access logs under its own policies.

Contact

For questions about information sent by email, use the contact shown on this site. This page describes the behavior of this version and should be updated when new integrations are introduced.
