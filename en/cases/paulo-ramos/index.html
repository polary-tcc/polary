Case Study · Paulo Ramos

Organize operations before adding complexity.

A management system that centralizes schedules, clients, services, products and financial information in a simple, usable experience.

01 · Context

The starting point.

The project started with the need to organize critical barbershop operations information in one centralized experience, preventing key routines from remaining scattered.

02 · Diagnosis

The problem was not “a lack of technology.”

Before thinking about the interface, the priority was to understand which information needed to be connected: schedules, clients, services, products and financial data. The system had to reduce friction, not create another layer of complexity.

03 · Strategy

Centralize what needs to work together.

The product direction was to organize the operation’s key areas into one structure, with clear hierarchy, predictable navigation and enough information to support daily work without overwhelming the user.

04 · Solution

A management system designed for real-world use.

The solution brings together schedules, clients, services, products and financial information tracking. The experience was designed to make recurring tasks more direct and the state of the operation easier to understand.

05 · Outcome

Results described without invented metrics.

The case demonstrates the ability to turn an operational need into an organized digital structure. Quantitative metrics are not presented until real, validated data is available.
